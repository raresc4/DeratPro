## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of scientific precision, biosafety, and pristine environmental restoration for professional sanitization, disinsection, and rodent control services (DDD). Rather than leveraging panic, visceral pests, or chaotic imagery, the visual narrative centers on clinical calm, structural integrity, and eco-certified safety. The interface evokes the confidence of a top-tier laboratory cross-pollinated with an architectural maintenance firm.

The design movement combines **Modern Technical Precision** with **Atmospheric Lightness**:
- **Purity & Sanitization**: Crisp canvas whites, ultra-subtle slate surfaces, and surgical spacing replace aggressive or cluttered utility aesthetics.
- **Architectural Security**: Controlled linear structure, discrete high-resolution line-art blueprints, and geometric micro-badges convey certification and reliability.
- **Zero-Alarmist Tone**: Communication relies entirely on botanical-safe iconography, verified metric seals, process step breakdowns, and structured intervention guarantees.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for clinical authority, biosafety certification, and uncompromising WCAG AAA/AA legibility across varied operational lighting environments:

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Oceanic Slate)**: Anchor for primary navigation, high-impact titles, key structural containers, and dominant CTAs. It communicates institutional authority, discretion, and technical certainty.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Botanical Emerald)**: Applied selectively for affirmative state indicators, eco-safe certification badges, quick-dispatch action triggers, and validated compliance markers. It signifies safety, environmental protection, and non-toxic domestic restoration.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Biosafety Cyan)**: Reserved for intervention status monitors, technical equipment certifications, water/air disinfection metrics, and interactive informational link states.
- **Neutral Palette**:
  - `Surface Clean`: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and input canvas.
  - `Surface Subtle`: `#F8FAFC` for global backdrops and structural section segmentation.
  - `Surface Muted`: `#F1F5F9` for secondary data wells, code snippets, and table headers.
  - `Border Soft`: `#E2E8F0` for hairline container dividers and accessible input boundaries.
  - `Text Muted`: `#64748B` for meta labels, contextual assistance, and secondary descriptions.
  - `Text Primary`: `#0F172A` for absolute reading clarity across all standard typography scales.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances geometric authority with inviting legibility:

- **Headlines (Manrope)**: Chosen for its geometric poise, modern industrial finish, and technical character. It establishes confident, reassuring credibility in hero titles and section headers without coming across as cold or bureaucratic.
- **Body & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, approachable readability across operational summaries, pricing structures, and intervention protocols. The slightly generous apertures ensure rapid legibility even under high-glare field conditions on mobile interfaces.
- **Optical Rules**: Display and hero variants use tighter negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) to unify word shapes. Micro-labels and technical certification codes enforce upper-cased tracking (`0.06em`) for surgical differentiation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column grid pinned to a strict 8-point vertical cadence, enforcing generous breathing room that projects tidiness, order, and hygiene.

- **Breakpoints & Containers**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1200px)`: 12 columns, max-width `1280px`, fluid outer margins, `24px` gutters.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1199px)`: 8 columns, `24px` outer canvas margins, `20px` gutters.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4 columns, `16px` outer canvas margins, `16px` gutters.
- **Composition Principles**:
  - Information density remains open and scannable. Primary process cards and quote configurators leverage generous `space-lg` and `space-xl` internal padding to prevent any feeling of visual congestion.
  - Vertical rhythm pairs related header elements tightly (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) while isolating unrelated intervention categories with wide structural gaps (`space-2xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects muddy, high-spread drops in favor of **Crisp Architectural Elevation** and **Atmospheric Tonal Sheen**:

- **Tier 0 (Base Surface)**: Flat `#F8FAFC` canvas. No shadow. Boundaries marked by crisp `#E2E8F0` structural hairlines.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Input Wells)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#0B192C` ambient tint:
  - `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`
  - Outlined by a soft 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Hovered Tiles)**:
  - `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`
  - Border transitions to `#CBD5E1` to simulate surface approach.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Intervention Dispatch Sheets, Urgent Overlays)**:
  - `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`
  - Paired with a soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) over a `#0B192C` 40% translucent veil.

## Shapes

The shape rhythm leverages purposeful soft curves to communicate human safety and hospitality, tempered by geometric edges that maintain technical rigor:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to buttons, text input fields, tooltips, and compact metric chips.
- **Medium Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Standard for operational cards, credential containers, service tiers, and pricing modules.
- **Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Applied to hero spotlight panels, callout banners, and modal dialog frameworks.
- **Full Radius (Pill)**: Reserved exclusively for small technical badges, status chips (`Active Protection`), and emergency telephone/call buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Emergency Intervention / Schedule)**:
  - Solid `#059669` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, `0.5rem` radius, `space-sm` vertical and `space-lg` horizontal padding.
  - Subtle inset top sheen `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Hover state darkens fill to `#047857` with slight elevation lift.
- **Secondary (Technical Specs / Protocol Download)**:
  - Solid `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, text `#0B192C`. Hover brings subtle background `#F8FAFC` and border `#94A3B8`.
- **Tertiary (Ghost / Dismiss)**:
  - Transparent fill, text `#64748B`, hover text `#0B192C` with background `#F1F5F9`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- `Eco-Safe Bio-Formula`: Light mint surface `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, paired with a 1.5px monoline leaf/shield icon.
- `ANPID Certified`: Subtle slate surface `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`.

### Input Fields
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, padding `space-md`.
- Typography `body-md` in `#0F172A`. Placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0284C7` accompanied by a 3px soft ring `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`. Zero visual vibration.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 20x20px with `4px` corner radius (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Default: Border 1.5px `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Border `#059669`, background `#059669` with a crisp geometric checkmark in white.

### Service & Diagnosis Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E2E8F0`, radius `1rem`, internal padding `space-xl`.
- Subtle top accent line (2px) in `#059669` (Eco/Sanitization) or `#0284C7` (Commercial Inspection).
- Imagery Rule: Exclusively high-key environmental photography (clean commercial kitchens, sunlit living rooms, laboratory test tubes) combined with monoline vector diagrams depicting target zones and perimeter buffers. Never display real or cartoon insects or rodents.

### Specialized Components
- **Intervention Timeline**: A vertical track in `#E2E8F0` populated by numeric circular milestones (`#0B192C`) indicating audit, target neutralization, safety clearance, and continuous post-monitoring.
- **Property Size & Risk Slider**: Sleek dual-tone interactive range slider with dynamic surface area calculation ($m^2$) and transparent chemical toxicity ratings (0% VOC indicator).
